# Product experience

## A reminder that understands place

A conventional reminder tells you to do something at a particular time. GeoNote addresses tasks that become relevant because you have arrived somewhere.

A typical use case is simple:

1. Create a reminder: "Return the library books."
2. Select the library using search or the map.
3. Keep the suggested alert radius or adjust it.
4. Go about your day.
5. Receive a local notification when the device detects entry into the configured area.

Reminders can also be paused, resumed, edited, and removed. The list and map represent the same collection, rather than two separate types of content.

## Beyond private reminders

GeoNote also includes location-based sharing and discovery:

- **Private:** a reminder remains personal.
- **Unlisted:** a reminder can be accessed through its shareable link without being listed for local discovery.
- **Public:** eligible reminders can appear in nearby discovery surfaces.
- **Local feed:** public reminders from other users are shown for the current area; the user's own reminders are excluded.

The discovery radius and a reminder's alert radius are distinct product concepts. This prevents decisions about browsing public content from changing when a private reminder should fire.

Publication and reporting flows include policy checks and moderation capabilities. Broad usage and operational behavior are still subject to post-deployment validation.

## Product principles

- Keep reminder creation fast, with useful defaults and optional advanced settings.
- Make maps functional rather than decorative.
- Give explicit feedback about permission and monitoring readiness.
- Keep personal reminders and public discovery separate.
- Treat mobile battery constraints and privacy boundaries as design inputs.
- Support English and Brazilian Portuguese.

## Product boundaries

GeoNote is a working pre-launch MVP, not a publicly distributed mobile service. The signed Android build has been tested on a real device; store distribution and wider device coverage remain future work. iOS is not currently a supported release target.
